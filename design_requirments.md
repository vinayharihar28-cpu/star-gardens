# Star Gardens — Visual Design Requirements

## Project objective
Transform the already-developed Star Gardens website into a distinctive, premium, visually rich landscape design experience. The current implementation is functional but visually too plain.

This is a redesign and refinement phase, not a request to blindly rebuild the entire project from scratch.

## Reference directions

### Humming Tree
- Use as inspiration for the opening logo reveal.
- Explore a subtle glow, light sweep or elegant logo transition.
- Transition naturally into the hero section.

### Landscape studio reference
- Use horizontal, image-led service panels.
- Give each service its own visual identity and interaction.
- Explore scroll-triggered reveals and hover states.

### Afterform
- Use as inspiration for restrained layouts, cinematic imagery, spacing and project storytelling.
- Avoid unnecessary decoration.

### Mosaic
- Use a dark, grid-based visual language.
- Explore black and charcoal backgrounds, multiple green tones, subtle texture and pixel-inspired details.
- Adapt the visual idea to botanical patterns, landscape plans and planting details rather than copying its product design.

## Proposed colour direction
- Obsidian: #101210
- Canopy green: #193D2B
- Fern green: #286746
- Moss: #60966C
- Sage: #A8C49A
- Botanical lime: #D9E7B0

Use the colours with restraint and contrast. Mix dark and lighter sections rather than making the entire website uniformly dark.

These are exploratory colours. Preserve any verified, client-approved branding requirements.

## Typography
- Use a refined, contemporary sans-serif for body copy and navigation.
- Consider a distinctive display typeface for major headings.
- Pixel-inspired typography may be used sparingly for labels, small numbers or decorative accents.
- Confirm the reference font's name and licensing before using it.

## Motion and interactions
- Short opening logo animation.
- Smooth transitions into the hero.
- Horizontal service panels with appropriate hover and scroll behaviour.
- Scroll-triggered reveals for selected sections.
- Subtle parallax or image movement where it improves storytelling.
- Carefully designed transitions between project sections.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences and maintain mobile performance.

## Design principles
- Premium, creative, botanical and architectural.
- Strong visual hierarchy and intentional composition.
- Large, high-quality landscape and project imagery.
- Rich visual detail without clutter.
- Functional navigation and clear enquiry actions.
- Mobile and desktop must both feel intentionally designed.

## Business accuracy
Use the existing website files and client-provided notes as the sources for business information. Do not invent services, project statistics, client names, testimonials or contact details.

Preserve working functionality unless a change is necessary and approved. Confirm the actual CRM destination before modifying Client Login.

## Final objective
Create a recognisable Star Gardens design identity that combines cinematic landscape storytelling, refined editorial layout, rich green-and-black art direction and purposeful scroll interactions.
